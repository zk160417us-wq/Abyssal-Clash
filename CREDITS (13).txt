Version 106 maps (made for Abyssal Clash, no outside models or textures - shapes and textures are generated by our own map maker):
  map_dust.glb   - DUST TOWN      (a walled desert town)
  map_pines.glb  - PINE VALLEY    (a forest valley with a waterfall)
  map_frost.glb  - FROST OUTPOST  (a snowy army outpost by a frozen lake)
  map_keep.glb   - STONE KEEP     (a castle)
  The themes (desert town, forest, snow, castle) were inspired by the classic maps of arena shooters; nothing was copied.
  The new first-person sleeves, gloves and reload animations are also our own work (on the existing fp_hands.glb).
